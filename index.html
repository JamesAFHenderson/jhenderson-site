Who this is for

Built for UK challenger drinks brands: founders scaling without senior commercial firepower in-house. If you need trade entry strategy, distributor and key-account management, pricing frameworks, or regular founder sparring on brand and commercial direction — I'm here.

The alternative is hiring a full-time senior commercial lead at £100k+ committed cost, signing with a distributor and giving away 25–30% gross margin in perpetuity, or attempting commercial scale-up without dedicated senior support and losing the year. This advisory sits between those at far lower outlay — and de-risks your B2B ambition significantly.

Also: foreign drinks brands ($3m–$50m revenue at home) evaluating UK entry, recently landed and underperforming, or post-Series A signalling international expansion.
